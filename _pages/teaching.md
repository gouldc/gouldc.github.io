## Mentorship

I love teaching and mentoring, and have worked with students and trainees at every level. I maintain close relationships with many past mentees, and continue several collaborations well beyond their training. My mentorship philosophy centers on meeting mentees where they are, both professionally and personally, and working together to define what success looks like for them. Because we start from that broad place, my mentorship relationships take different forms &mdash; some are centered on a specific research project, others are much broader in scope.

I've mentored PhD, master's, and undergraduate researchers at Columbia, Stanford, and UC San Diego. At UCSD, I have chaired Master's theses, advised PhD practicums, and served on dissertation committees.
